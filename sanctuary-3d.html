Sanctuary is a birch desk calendar made to be given. Twelve months of paper, held by a board that outlasts them.

Every piece slots in by hand, no tools and no glue.

02 the board

Four parts, and nothing extra.

Birch, brass and paper, cut and finished to sit together. Every piece slots in by hand, no tools and no glue.

  • Birch board, 10 × 15 in, 4 mm thick
  • Ledge plate: slots in to make the ledge and the foot
  • Peg: holds the month in place
  • Sheets: a month to a sheet
  • Wall plate: swaps in when it hangs
  • Brass plate on the back: hangs it flat on a wall

02 desk or wall

On the desk, or on the wall.

Slide the table plate out and the wall plate in. A brass plate on the back lets it hang flat.

03 one sheet, two sides

Plan on the front. Reflect on the back.

The front is the month, in weekly rows with room for each day’s to-dos. Turn it over and the same sheet is a journal page.

or drag the sheet
