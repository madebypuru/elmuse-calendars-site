What's the difference between the three?

Sanctuary is a birch board for your desk or wall, with a sheet for every month. Polaroid is a set of stamp cards you carry behind your phone cover. Distinctive is a 3 by 2 ft wall planner that shows the year at a glance.

What's on the back of each Sanctuary month?

A journal page you can write on, a postcard you can tear off and send, and a QR code for that month.

What does the QR code on Sanctuary do?

It opens that month's resources. They unlock at midnight on the 1st, so each month has something new waiting.

Does Sanctuary go on a desk or a wall?

Either. It stands on a desk or hangs on a wall.

How do the Polaroid cards stay on my phone?

Each card slips behind a clear phone cover, so you see this month's card every time you pick up your phone. If you'd rather not use a cover, you can add a MagSafe holder.

Which Distinctive should I choose?

It comes in green or ivory, horizontal or vertical. Horizontal reads like a timeline. Vertical fits a narrower wall.

Can we order in bulk, and what does it cost?

Yes. We quote every team order, because the price depends on the calendars, the numbers and what you'd like changed. Tell us what you need and we'll send a quote.

Can you put our logo on the calendars?

On Sanctuary, we laser-engrave your logo on the birch board, and the artwork, monthly themes and monthly QR can all be yours. On Distinctive, we can make custom artwork for larger orders. On Polaroid, we add your brand stickers.

Can our clients and our team get different versions?

Yes. We can make a separate edition for your clients and another for your team.
