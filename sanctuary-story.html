      elmuse · the 2027 calendar

      The Sanctuary Calendar

      A year that moves at a kinder pace. Twelve loose sheets on a birch board, for the desks of the people you work with.

      01 The board

      Birch, made to stay on the desk.

      1. 1 A birch-wood board
      2. 2 A ledge the sheets rest on
      3. 3 A wooden peg to hold them
      4. 4 A band below for your logo

      It stands on a desk or hangs on a wall.

      02 Your name in the wood

      Burnt in, not printed.

      Your logo is laser-engraved into the birch below the ledge. Type your company’s name and watch it burn in.

      03 One sheet, two sides

      Plan on the front. Reflect on the back.

      The front is the month, in weekly rows with room for each day’s to-dos. Turn it over and the same sheet is a journal page.

      04 The journal · January

      A reflection to open the month.

      A short piece written for the month’s theme, to read with the first cup of tea.

      04 The journal · January

      A question to sit with.

      A “reflect” prompt, with lines to answer it in your own hand.

      04 The journal · January

      A small ritual.

      One slow, simple thing to do this month. Beside it, a QR with more.

      05 The QR on the 1st

      At midnight, the month opens.

      Scan the “more for you” QR. On the 1st, it unlocks that month’s playlists, recipes, book lists and places.

      05 For companies

      Or let it speak for you.

      Point the same QR to your own page each month: a note from the founders, team news, a thank-you. Your team runs it, or we do.

      06 Twelve themes · 01 / 12

      JanuaryOpen Skies

      “your sign to look up”

      Each month has its own theme. The artwork, the journal and the QR all follow it.

      07 The postcard

      The picture tears off.

      The artwork strip is perforated. Drag it up to tear it off, or keep scrolling.

      07 The postcard

      Turn it over: a real postcard.

      Room on the back for a note, an address and a stamp.

      07 The postcard

      Send it, or keep it.

      Post it to someone who needs it this month, or keep it for yourself.

      07 By December

      Twelve kept cards, one wall of art.

      The postcards gather over the year into a small gallery of 2027.

      08 The year closes

      Twelve frames, and a letter to the year.

      The last sheet keeps “moments that felt like a sanctuary” in twelve photo frames. On its back, a letter to 2027.

      09 Made for your team

      Yours, from the wood to the QR.

      • Your logo engraved on every board.
      • Your artwork and themes, or ours made for you. Supplying your own costs less.
      • Your monthly QR, run by your team or by us, for the year or month by month.
      • Two editions: one for clients, one for your team.
